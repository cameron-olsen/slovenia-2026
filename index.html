Tips

🚗
Car & Driving
Karavanke Tunnel toll ~€12 northbound — keep card or cash handy. Confirm the rental includes a Slovenian motorway vignette. Ljubljana is traffic-free in the centre — park at the InterContinental (valet recommended) and walk everywhere. Download offline Slovenia maps before departure.
💶
Money
Slovenia uses the Euro. Cards accepted almost everywhere except very small stalls. ATMs available in Bled and Ljubljana; Kranjska Gora has one in the centre. Amex Platinum's travel protections cover Slovenia — use it as your primary card.
🌡️
Weather in Late June / July
Highs of 26–32°C / 79–90°F in Ljubljana. Cooler in the mountains at 18–24°C / 64–75°F. Afternoon thunderstorms are common in July — pack a light waterproof. Lake temperatures are excellent for swimming by late June (~21°C / ~70°F). Bring sunscreen for the water.
🏔️
Lake Bled Practicalities
Park at Mlino village (southern shore) for Mala Osojnica — better than the main car park. Vintgar Gorge entry is 8:20–8:40 AM (booked) — arrive a few minutes early. Kremna rezina (Bled cream cake) at Park Restaurant on the lakefront — a local institution, don't leave without one.
🏰
Predjama Castle
Arrive exactly at 10 AM to get in before tour groups. The audio tour is genuinely good — don't skip it. The cave system accessible from the courtyard is worth exploring. Leave Ljubljana by 9:00 AM for comfortable timing before the JAZ dinner.
🍷
Food & Wine
Try Rebula and Malvazija whites from the Goriška Brda region. Teran red from the Kras plateau. Kremna rezina — Bled's vanilla cream slice. Potica — traditional rolled nut cake. Kranjska klobasa — Carniolan sausage, best with mustard. Laško and Union are the local lagers.
🏛️
Ljubljana Tips
Entirely walkable from the InterContinental — 10 min on foot to everything. BicikeLJ city bikes: stations throughout the centre, first hour free. Metelkova arts district (former Yugoslav military barracks) is worth an evening wander. Robba Fountain and St Nicholas Cathedral are on the same short walk.
📸
Flytographer Shoot — Fri 3 Jul
Photographer Mankica · 60 min · 35 photos · Old Town route. Shoot starts ~6:30 PM (likely runs a little later). Meet at the Ljubljana Tourist Information Centre on the Stritarjeva / Prešernov trg corner near the Triple Bridge — arrive 10 min early. Sunset in Ljubljana on 3 July is ~8:45 PM, so the light will still be warm and slanting. Wear something photo-ready. Late = forfeit time; extra time is $165 per 30 min if Mankica is available.
🗣️
Language
Slovenian is the official language. English is widely spoken in hotels and tourist areas. A few phrases go a long way: Prosim (please), Hvala (thank you), Dober dan (good day). Menus will almost always have English.
🎒
What to Pack
Good walking shoes for Mala Osojnica and Vintgar Gorge. Swimwear — Lake Bled and Lake Bohinj. Light waterproof jacket for afternoon mountain storms. EU power adapter (type C/F). Sunscreen — essential on the water. Something nice for the JAZ dinner and the photo shoot.
