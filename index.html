Tips

🚗
Car Rental
AVIS picked up and dropped at Marco Polo. Confirm Slovenia cross-border coverage is explicitly stated. Buy the Slovenian motorway vignette (~€15/week) at the border or first petrol station. Download offline Google Maps for Slovenia before you go.
🌡️
Weather in July
25–32°C in Ljubljana and Bled. Mountains 18–24°C. Afternoon thunderstorms common in the Julian Alps — check forecast before heading out. Bring a light jacket for mountain evenings. Sun protection essential on the water.
🏔️
Lake Bled Practicalities
Park at Mlino village (southern shore) for Mala Osojnica — better than the main car park. Lake water is 20–22°C all summer. Bled cream cake (kremna rezina) at Smon Confectionery is worth stopping for. Hit Vintgar at 8 AM — it gets very busy by 10 AM.
🏰
Predjama Castle
Arrive at 10 AM exactly — you'll get in before tour groups. The audio tour is excellent; don't skip it. There's a small cave system accessible from the courtyard. Allow 1.5 hours. Leave Ljubljana by 9:00 AM sharp.
🍷
Food & Wine
Slovenia produces world-class wine that's almost never exported. Try Rebula and Malvazija whites. Try Teran red from the Kras plateau. JAZ on Saturday is the centrepiece. The Milka breakfast Monday morning is not to be missed — set your alarm.
🏛️
Ljubljana Tips
Entirely walkable from the InterContinental. Pogačar market open Tue–Sat until ~6 PM. The Plečnikove tržnice covered market hall is an architectural gem worth exploring. Metelkova arts district — former Yugoslav military barracks — is worth an evening wander.
💶
Money
Both Slovenia and Italy use the Euro. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Keep some cash for Pogačar market, pletna boats (~€18pp), and small entry fees like Predjama. ATMs widely available in Ljubljana and Bled.
🎒
What to Pack
Good walking shoes for Mala Osojnica and Vintgar Gorge. Swimwear — Lake Bled, Lake Bohinj. Light jacket for mountain evenings. Old clothes for rafting day — wetsuits are provided but you'll get wet before putting them on. Small day pack for hike days.
